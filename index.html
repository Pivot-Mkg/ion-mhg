Products & Solutions

Engineering

IonExchange provides complete water and wastewater treatment solution, including desalination, reuse and water-to-energy for industries, institutions and municipalities.

Resins

The resin range from Ion Exchange (India) offers high-performance ion-exchange beads in dry and moist formats tailored for diverse sectors like pharma, food, nuclear and hydrometallurgy.

Membranes

The membranes from Ion Exchange (India)’s HYDRAMEM range offer cutting-edge, high-performance membrane solutions for industrial, institutional and domestic water-treatment applications.

Speciality Chemicals

The speciality-chemicals offering from Ion Exchange (India) features customised treatment programmes including boiler/cooling water chemicals, antiscalants and membrane cleaning solutions for industrial use.

Instrument Automation

The instrument and automation solutions from Ion Exchange (India) Ltd. deliver industrial-grade monitoring and control systems for water quality, effluent and process parameters.
